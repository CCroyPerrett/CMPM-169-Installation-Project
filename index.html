CMPM 169 Installation Project




Immersive Input: Utilizizing a camera, this system allows those pictured by the camera to wave around colored objects as to paint their movement over time onto the canvas. To make such input more immersive, we could hand out a series of colored "magic wands" to the audience, with their specially colored tips allowing them to paint across the canvas as they move through the air. You can use nearby colorful objects to paint on the canvas as well!

Planned Production: By setting up a larger display in a space that people are passing by through, would allow for a reliable stream of foot traffic. All the hardware this project requries is one large screen and a quality camera, although a series of specially colored "wands" to hand out to viewers would also be a helpful addition, as to allow for the more easy painting for a variety of colors (which in all may cost a few hundred). Between art meuseum exhibits would be a functional place for this display a distraction for passer-bys to play with momentarily as a break between relevantly-themed destinations. More wizardly set dressing would be a fun inclusion as well, as to sell the whole "magic canvas" vibe!
